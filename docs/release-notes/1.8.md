## Usage history — the part 1.7 promised

1.7 quietly started recording your usage. 1.8 is the release where you can actually read it.

Under each bar is now **one bar per completed limit window**, oldest on the left, with a faint full-height slot behind each so you can see what you *didn't* use. Windows you maxed out draw in red.

The point isn't nostalgia, it's the question nobody can answer from a single number: **am I on the right plan?** A caption underneath says which way it's leaning — *"last 12 · maxed 1×"* if you're occasionally hitting the ceiling, *"last 11 · ~83% unused"* if you're paying for headroom you never touch.

Juicage records the peak of each window rather than every reading, so a year of history is a small readable JSON file on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

> If you installed 1.7 and saw nothing, that was expected — the chart needs two *closed* windows before it can say anything. About ten hours for sessions; a fortnight for weekly caps.

## Today's sessions

A strip across the bottom shows **when today's session windows actually opened and closed**, on a midnight-to-midnight axis with a line marking now.

This is the thing that's genuinely hard to see otherwise: a session window is a rolling five hours anchored to whenever it *opened*, not a fixed clock. Start work an hour before one closes and you get an hour of it, not five. The strip shows the difference between a day you used well and a day you kept starting sessions you couldn't finish.

The reset line says so too now: **"Opened 12:10 PM · resets 5:10 PM"** rather than just the reset time.

## Easy to miss: local models

Not new in this release, but worth pointing at since it's what most people don't realise Juicage does. When **Ollama or LM Studio** is actively generating, a green dot appears in the menu bar and the popover names the model, the engine, and how long it's been running — with a notification when the job finishes. It's the difference between watching a terminal and getting on with something else.

## Fixes

- **Windows were being counted several times.** claude.ai reports a window's reset time a second or two apart between polls — 4:59:59 on one, 5:00:00 on the next — and each variant was recorded as its own window. One week could appear four or five times over, which made "last 8" show the same week repeatedly.
- **History could lose its most recent entry when Juicage quit.** Writes were batched a couple of seconds out with nothing forcing them at exit — and Sparkle quits the app to install an update, so the update itself was the most likely moment to clip the tail off a history that can't be rebuilt.
- **The pace line no longer gets clipped** mid-sentence in the popover.

> **Your history will look shorter after upgrading, and that's the fix working.** Duplicate windows are merged on load, so a count that read 11 might settle to 4. Nothing was lost — those extra entries were the same window wearing different hats.
