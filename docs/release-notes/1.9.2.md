## Easier-to-read small text

The small gray text in the popover — pace lines, reset times, history captions, the day strip's hours, "Updated just now" — was too faint, especially over a light desktop. It's now noticeably darker in both light and dark mode, while staying quieter than the headings.

Download the `.dmg`, open it, and drag Juicage onto Applications. Or `brew upgrade --cask juicage`.
