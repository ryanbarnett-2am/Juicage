## Fixes a wrong colour in the history chart

The bars under each limit are meant to say one thing with colour: **red means that window hit the cap.** In 1.8 they also picked up the colour of the metric's *current* state — so a session that was forecast to run out repainted its entire history red, including windows that finished at 20%.

The effect was a chart telling you that you'd maxed out repeatedly when you hadn't, and it only appeared while a limit was alerting — exactly when you'd be looking at it.

History now shows what happened in each window, independent of what's happening right now.
