## More than one claude.ai account

If you sign in to Claude with more than one account — a personal one, a work one, a team you administer — Juicage can now watch all of them at once. Each keeps its own session, and each account's limits appear separately in the popover.

**Preferences → Accounts** to add, rename or remove one. Adding an account opens the normal claude.ai sign-in window; Juicage never handles a password. Removing one signs it out on this Mac and takes its cookies with it, while keeping the history it recorded.

Your existing sign-in is untouched by the upgrade.

> Separate sign-ins need **macOS 14 or later** — the isolated sessions they rely on don't exist on Ventura, where Juicage stays single-account rather than letting two logins overwrite each other.

## Juicage was using far too much memory

It was holding around **800 MB**. It now uses about **150 MB**.

The cause: Juicage reads your usage through a hidden web view, and that view was loading the whole of claude.ai — the entire web app, resident in memory all day — in order to make two small API calls. It only ever needed the *address*, not the page. It now loads an empty one.

Found by [@traisjames](https://github.com/traisjames), who noticed the app was holding far more than it had any business holding and filed [#14](https://github.com/ryanbarnett-2am/Juicage/issues/14) with the evidence. Tested across 27 refresh cycles to confirm it stays flat rather than merely starting smaller.

## Choose what the menu bar shows

Two additions here, and the first one isn't mine.

**Which limits appear as text**, contributed by [@tibuntu](https://github.com/tibuntu) in [#13](https://github.com/ryanbarnett-2am/Juicage/pull/13). Tick the limits you want beside the ring, and choose how much of each name to carry — full words, initials (`C:31% W:35% F:66%`), or bare percentages for a menu bar that's short on room.

**Which limits get a ring.** Up to three, so a per-model cap like Fable can have one of its own. They draw outermost-first in the order the popover lists them, so the outer ring keeps meaning your current session. The default is unchanged — if you don't go looking, nothing moves.

## Smaller things

- **Times follow your region.** 12-hour with AM/PM or 24-hour, according to your macOS setting instead of a hardcoded American format.
- **A claude.ai outage no longer hides your usage.** Juicage used to replace the rings with a status dot, which threw away the number you still needed — degraded service doesn't stop you spending. The rings stay, and a small badge appears in the corner instead.

Download the `.dmg`, open it, and drag Juicage onto Applications. Or `brew upgrade --cask juicage`.
