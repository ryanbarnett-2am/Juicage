## Plans without a 5-hour session

If your plan no longer has a session limit, you could lose a ring, or on the default settings the percentage beside it. Now a limit that goes away hands its ring and its menu bar spot to the next limit down, and takes them back if it returns.

## Fewer local-model notifications

The notification setting in **Preferences → Local Models** is now a choice: never, or only when a job ran for at least 1, 5 or 15 minutes. If you had notifications on, you start at 1 minute. The green dot works as before.

## Mission Control on macOS 27

With Juicage running, Mission Control showed no windows for any app. Fixed by [@tibuntu](https://github.com/tibuntu) in [#15](https://github.com/ryanbarnett-2am/Juicage/pull/15).

Download the `.dmg`, open it, and drag Juicage onto Applications. Or `brew upgrade --cask juicage`.
